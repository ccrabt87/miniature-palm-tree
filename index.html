A Note on Scope

This workbook makes you a more present, more understandable nurse at the bedside. It does not qualify you to interpret, and it says so on every page where it matters. Consent, diagnosis, prognosis, code status, and discharge teaching all require a qualified medical interpreter — Chapter 8 draws that line in detail. Everything else is fair game, and that territory is larger than most nurses realize.
