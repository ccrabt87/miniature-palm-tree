# Zeros to Heroes - Tryptych Dice Roller

**An elegant, powerful dice roller for your Zeros to Heroes TTRPG sessions.**

## What is it?

The Tryptych Dice Roller brings the unique three-axis rolling system of Zeros to Heroes to life with a beautiful, intuitive interface. Whether you're a player tracking your hero's epic moments or a GM managing multiple NPCs, this tool makes rolling fast, visual, and satisfying.

## How It Works

Every roll uses three axes:
- **Stat Axis** (-5 to +5): Your character's skill or aptitude
- **Context Axis** (-3 to +3): Your current position or advantage
- **Symbolic Axis** (-2 to +3): The narrative weight of the moment

Roll 3d6, add your modifiers, and instantly see your outcome:
- **Miss** (≤5): Things go wrong
- **Strained Success** (6-9): You succeed, but at a cost
- **Clean Success** (10-13): You nail it
- **Surpassing Success** (≥14): Legendary achievement
