How to Use This Workbook

Twenty minutes a day, four or five days a week, will get you further than a weekend cram. Clinical Spanish is a performance skill: you learn it out loud.

Three ways in

If you have…Do this
A shift tomorrowPrint the Pocket Card (Ch. 12) and the Emergency Flashcards (Ch. 5). Read Ch. 8 so you know when to stop and call the interpreter.
Eight weeksFollow the plan in Appendix D, one chapter per week, and log every real-patient attempt.
Some Spanish alreadyTake the quizzes in Ch. 10 cold. Study only the chapters where you scored under 80%.

The study loop

  1. Hear it. Read each Spanish line aloud using the pronunciation guide. Record yourself on your phone and play it back.
  2. Say it to a person. A colleague, a friend, the mirror. Clinical phrases have to come out under stress, so practise standing up.
  3. Use one new phrase per shift. Greetings and comfort phrases first. Note how it went in the log (Appendix D).
  4. Test yourself. Every chapter has a quiz in Ch. 10. Fill in your answers on screen or on paper, then check Ch. 11.

Filling in this PDF

Every underlined blank is a fillable form field. Open the PDF in Adobe Acrobat Reader (free), Foxit, or a browser and click any line to type. Print the same pages blank if you prefer pencil. Save often: some browser viewers do not keep your typing unless you download the file first.
