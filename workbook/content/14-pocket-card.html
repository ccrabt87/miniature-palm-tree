Chapter Twelve

Pocket Reference Card

One hundred phrases, two sides, badge-reel sized. This is the only page in the book designed to be used without reading — the phrase you need should be findable by category in under three seconds.

How to make it

  1. Print the next two pages single-sided on card stock, or on plain paper if that is what you have.
  2. Cut on the solid border.
  3. Glue or tape back-to-back so Side A and Side B face outward, then laminate — a self-sealing pouch works, and so does clear packing tape on both sides.
  4. Punch a hole in the top corner and put it on your badge reel.

One line to add before you laminate

Write your interpreter line and access code across the top of Side B in permanent marker. It is the number you will need fastest and the one you are least likely to remember at 3 a.m.

Interpreter line / access code

The card's limits, printed on purpose

Nothing on this card is for consent, diagnosis, prognosis, code status, discharge teaching, or breaking bad news. Those are interpreter conversations every time. This card exists so a patient is not alone in the meantime.

Greeting & orientation

Hello, I’m your nurse.
Hola, soy su enfermero(a).

What is your name?
¿Cómo se llama?

Date of birth?
¿Fecha de nacimiento?

You are in the hospital.
Está en el hospital.

I’m going to take care of you.
Lo(a) voy a cuidar.

Do you speak English?
¿Habla inglés?

We have free interpreters.
Tenemos intérpretes gratis.

I’ll call one right now.
Voy a llamar a uno ahora mismo.

Do you understand? — say it back
¿Me lo puede decir con sus palabras?

I’ll be back soon.
Regreso pronto.

Do you need anything?
¿Necesita algo?

More slowly, please.
Más despacio, por favor.

Pain & assessment

Where does it hurt?
¿Dónde le duele?

Point to where it hurts.
Señale dónde le duele.

Does it hurt here?
¿Le duele aquí?

Zero to ten, how bad?
¿Del cero al diez, qué número?

When did it start?
¿Cuándo empezó?

Does it spread anywhere?
¿Se corre a otra parte?

What makes it worse?
¿Qué lo empeora?

Do you feel nauseated?
¿Tiene náuseas?

Are you dizzy?
¿Está mareado(a)?

Short of breath?
¿Le falta el aire?

Do you have a fever?
¿Tiene fiebre?

Any itching or rash?
¿Tiene comezón o ronchas?

Are you allergic to anything?
¿Es alérgico(a) a algo?

Instructions

Lie down, please.
Acuéstese, por favor.

Sit up, please.
Siéntese, por favor.

Turn on your side.
Voltéese de lado.

Take a deep breath.
Respire hondo.

Hold your breath.
Aguante la respiración.

Open your mouth.
Abra la boca.

Squeeze my hands.
Apriete mis manos.

Don’t move.
No se mueva.

Relax.
Relájese.

Don’t get up alone.
No se levante solo(a).

Press this button and I’ll come.
Apriete este botón y vengo.

Nothing to eat or drink.
Nada de comer ni beber.

May I touch here?
¿Puedo tocar aquí?

Comfort

I’m right here.
Aquí estoy.

You’re safe.
Está seguro(a).

This will be quick.
Va a ser rápido.

A small poke.
Un piquete pequeño.

Almost done.
Ya casi terminamos.

You’re doing well.
Lo está haciendo bien.

I’m sorry you’re going through this.
Siento mucho que esté pasando por esto.

Tell me if you’re cold.
Dígame si le da frío.

I’m going to cover you.
Lo(a) voy a tapar.

Take your time.
Tómese su tiempo.

Your family knows you’re here.
Su familia sabe que está aquí.

Thank you for your patience.
Gracias por su paciencia.
