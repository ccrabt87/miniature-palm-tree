Discharge Instructions

Eight bilingual templates you can fill in on screen and print for the patient. Spanish for the patient is in the large type; the English headings are for you.

Before you hand one over
  1. Your facility must approve these templates. Many organisations require professionally translated discharge documents; ask your language-access office.
  2. Fill every blank from the provider’s orders and the medication reconciliation, not from memory.
  3. Discharge teaching goes through a qualified interpreter, with teach-back. Hand the sheet over as well as, not instead of, that conversation.
  4. Ask if the patient reads Spanish (¿Lee en español?). If not, the conversation is everything; consider a recorded or pictorial option.

Lo que debe hacer en casa · At-home instructions
