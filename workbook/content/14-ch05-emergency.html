Chapter 5

Emergency Response Flashcards

Forty-eight short commands and questions for the first minutes of an emergency, when there is no time to wait for an interpreter. Call the interpreter as soon as the patient is stable.

How to use these cardsPrint these pages, cut along the outer dashed lines, and fold each card along the centre line so English is on one side and Spanish on the other. Punch a corner and keep them on a badge reel. Drill the English side until the Spanish comes without thinking.
SafetyOverhead code names (“Code Blue”, “Rapid Response”) and some phrases here, such as giving aspirin, depend on your facility’s protocols and the provider’s orders. These cards are for talking to the patient; they do not replace your protocols.
