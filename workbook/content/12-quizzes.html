Chapter Ten

Self-Assessment Quizzes

Five tests, seventy questions. Take each one before you look at Chapter 11, and say the Spanish answers out loud rather than only writing them — a phrase you can write but not pronounce is a phrase you do not have.

Scoring yourself honestly

Anything below 80 percent on a test means re-read the chapter, not push on to the next one. Mark the questions you guessed correctly as wrong — a lucky guess at the desk is a blank at the bedside.
