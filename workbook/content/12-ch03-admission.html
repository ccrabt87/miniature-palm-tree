Chapter 3

The Admission Interview

Sixty questions in the order most admission screens ask them. Nearly all can be answered with yes/no, a number, or a short word, so you can understand the answer as well as ask the question.

SafetyAllergies, home medications, code status and advance directives are high-stakes. If the patient’s answer is anything but a clear “no”, or you are not certain you understood, confirm through a qualified interpreter before you document it.

Culture noteQuestions 50–53 and 56–58 touch on shame, money and immigration fears. Ask them privately, explain why you ask (Le hacemos estas preguntas a todos los pacientes — we ask every patient these questions), and use an interpreter for any positive answer. Never use a family member to interpret a domestic-safety question.
