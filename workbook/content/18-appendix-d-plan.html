If a week gets away from you

Do not restart. Move the date and keep going — the order matters more than the calendar. The one week worth repeating rather than skipping is Week 1, because every later week depends on being able to read a word aloud correctly the first time you meet it.

Phrase log

Every shift, write down one phrase you needed and did not have. Look it up, check it with a native speaker or an interpreter, and drill it. Twenty-four of these is a vocabulary built entirely out of your own gaps — worth more than any list somebody else wrote.

Finished

Eight weeks of a book is not a credential, and this page is not a certificate — formal medical-interpreter qualification is a different process, run by your facility or a certifying body, and worth asking about if this work suited you. What this page is: a record that you spent two months getting better at the part of nursing that does not show up on a flowsheet.
